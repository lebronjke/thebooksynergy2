Book Promotion

A launch strategy built around your audience, budget, and timeline — so the right readers actually hear about your book.

What it is

A plan, not just a checklist of tactics

Book promotion means building a realistic plan for reaching your target readers — social content, mailing list strategy, ARC distribution, or ad spend, depending on what actually fits your book and budget. It's tailored to where you are, not a one-size-fits-all launch template.

How it works

From strategy to launch day

Step 1

Tell us about your book

Share your genre, audience, launch date, and budget range.

Step 2

Strategy call

We talk through what's realistic and what will actually move the needle for your book.

Step 3

Campaign plan

You receive a written plan with a timeline, channels, and content approach.

Step 4

Execution support

We help execute or hand off a plan you can run yourself — your choice.

What's included

Every promotion package covers

  • A strategy call to map your audience and goals
  • A written launch plan with timeline and channel recommendations
  • Guidance on ARC distribution and early review outreach
  • Content direction for social and mailing list touchpoints
  • Optional hands-on execution support around launch week
