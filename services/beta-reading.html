Service 03

Author Support

Ongoing guidance through revision rounds, so feedback from beta readers or editors turns into a clear next draft — not a pile of notes you're not sure how to use.

What it is

A steady hand through the revision process

Feedback is only useful if you know what to do with it. Author Support is ongoing guidance — helping you prioritize notes, plan revision rounds, and stay on track between drafts. It's less a one-time service and more a working relationship through the messier stretch of finishing a book.

How it works

From notes to next draft

Step 1

Tell us where you're stuck

Share your manuscript status and the feedback or notes you're working from.

Step 2

We build a plan

Notes get organized into a prioritized revision plan with realistic milestones.

Step 3

Regular check-ins

Scheduled check-ins keep revision moving and catch new issues early.

Step 4

You reach the next draft

Support continues until you have a draft you're confident sending onward.

What's included

Every author support package covers

  • A prioritized revision plan built from your existing feedback
  • Scheduled check-in calls across your revision timeline
  • Help triaging conflicting notes from multiple readers or editors
  • Accountability on milestones, without rigid deadlines
  • Direct access to ask questions between check-ins
