Service 01

Beta Reading

Real readers respond to your unpublished manuscript the way your eventual audience will — so you find out what's working, and what isn't, before the book goes public.

What it is

Honest reactions from real readers

A beta reader isn't an editor or proofreader. They read your book as a genuine reader would, and tell you where the story pulled them in, where it lost them, and whether the characters and plot actually land. That reaction is something you can't get by rereading your own draft.

How it works

From submission to notes

Step 1

Submit your manuscript

Send your draft along with your genre, word count, and what kind of feedback you want most.

Step 2

We match readers

Your book is paired with readers who genuinely enjoy your genre and read at your target audience's level.

Step 3

They read and note

Readers work through the manuscript over your chosen timeline, tracking reactions as they go.

Step 4

You get structured feedback

Notes come back organized by chapter and theme — pacing, characters, clarity — ready to act on.

What's included

Every beta read covers

  • 2–3 beta readers matched to your genre and audience
  • Chapter-by-chapter reaction notes as they read
  • A summary report covering pacing, character believability, and plot clarity
  • Direct quotes on moments that worked and moments that didn't
  • A follow-up call or written Q&A to clarify any notes
