Service 06

Press & Media / Book Release

Press outreach, media lists, and a coordinated plan for release day — so your book's launch gets real visibility, not just a single announcement post.

What it is

Coordinated coverage for launch day and beyond

This service handles the press and media side of a book release — drafting a press release, identifying relevant outlets, bloggers, and podcasts in your genre, and building a timeline so outreach happens with enough lead time to land coverage around your actual release date.

How it works

From press kit to release day

Step 1

Share your release details

Tell us your release date, genre, and any press or media goals you have in mind.

Step 2

Press kit built

A press release, author bio, and book summary are drafted for outreach use.

Step 3

Media list assembled

Relevant outlets, bloggers, and podcasts in your genre are identified and prioritized.

Step 4

Outreach and release timeline

Outreach goes out on a schedule timed around your release date for maximum coverage.

What's included

Every press & media package covers

  • A drafted press release and author bio ready for outreach
  • A curated media list of outlets, bloggers, and podcasts in your genre
  • A release-day timeline coordinating outreach and announcements
  • Guidance on responding to press inquiries as they come in
  • A post-release summary of coverage and outreach results
