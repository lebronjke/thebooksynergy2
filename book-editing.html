Service 04

Book Editing

Line-level and copy editing that tightens your prose, fixes grammar and consistency issues, and keeps your voice fully intact.

What it is

Sentence-level polish, not a rewrite

Book editing focuses on the sentence and paragraph level — grammar, clarity, word choice, consistency in names and timelines, and rhythm. This is the pass that comes after the big structural questions are settled, when it's time to make every sentence read as cleanly as it can.

How it works

From draft to polished manuscript

Step 1

Submit your manuscript

Send your draft and let us know if you want a sample edit first.

Step 2

Sample edit (optional)

We edit a short sample chapter so you can see the approach before committing.

Step 3

Full edit pass

Your editor works through the manuscript with tracked changes and margin notes.

Step 4

You review and approve

You receive the edited file plus a short summary of recurring patterns to watch for.

What's included

Every editing pass covers

  • Grammar, punctuation, and spelling corrections throughout
  • Consistency checks for names, timelines, and details
  • Line-level suggestions for clarity and rhythm, tracked for your review
  • A short report on recurring patterns in your writing
  • One round of follow-up questions after you review the edit
