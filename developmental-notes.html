Service 02

Developmental Notes

Structural feedback on story arc, chapter balance, and where reader interest rises or drops — the big-picture notes that shape a strong revision.

What it is

Feedback on the architecture of your story

Developmental notes go beyond a single reader's reaction — they look at the shape of the whole manuscript. Is the story arc balanced? Does the middle sag? Are subplots pulling their weight? This is the level of feedback that helps you revise with a clear plan, not just a list of moments to fix.

How it works

From manuscript to structural map

Step 1

Submit your manuscript

Send your draft along with a short note on what stage of revision you're in.

Step 2

Full structural read

An editor reads the entire manuscript, mapping arc, pacing, and chapter balance.

Step 3

Notes are compiled

Findings are organized into a clear report — what's working structurally, and what needs attention.

Step 4

Review call

A short call walks through the notes together, so nothing is left ambiguous.

What's included

Every developmental read covers

  • A full-manuscript structural read by one editor
  • A written report covering story arc, pacing, and chapter balance
  • Notes on subplot strength and whether every thread earns its place
  • A revision roadmap — prioritized, not just a list of issues
  • One follow-up call to walk through the notes
